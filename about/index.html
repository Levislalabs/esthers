About Esther’s
Sheet Metal

A sheet metal shop in Burnaby, British Columbia, fabricating architectural metalwork since 1995.

What We Do

Custom, not catalogue

We make architectural sheet metal to order: chimney caps, flashing, coping and roof accessories in copper, zinc, aluminum and painted steel.

Most of what leaves the shop started as a drawing somebody brought us. A chimney cap that fits one chase will not fit the next one along, so the dimensions, the louvre spacing and the crown profile are set per job rather than picked from a range.

Two Shops

Both in Burnaby

The two locations do different work, and it is worth knowing which one you want before you pick up the phone.

01

Main Branch — 3890 E. First Ave.

Standard and custom flashing, and specialty chimney caps including louvered and French-curved designs. Also the sheet metal essentials that go with the job: matching paint, screws and caulking.

02

Specialty Shop — 3701 Keith Street

Specialty fabrication: chimney caps, vents, curved flashing, arched-window work and copper scuppers, among other custom sheet metal projects.
