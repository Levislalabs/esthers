Copper

A finish that keeps working after you leave

Copper is the only material here that is not finished when it is installed. Scrub the timeline, or press play, to watch a standing seam cap move from mill bright through russet and chocolate brown to full verdigris.

Weathering rate depends on rainfall, orientation, air chemistry and how much of the surface is sheltered. Coastal and industrial exposures reach verdigris faster; a protected face under a deep overhang can stay brown for decades. Pre-patinated copper is available where matching an existing installation matters more than watching the change.

Zinc

How It Works

From measurement to roof

Custom work goes wrong in the handover, not the fabrication. This is the order we do things in, and what we need from you at each step.

01

Measure

Send us site measurements or a detail drawing. For chimney work we need the outside dimensions at the crown, the flue count and heights, and the roof pitch. If you would rather we measured it, say so on the quote request.

02

Specify

Pick the material and gauge, then the colour. The configurator on this page exists for exactly this step. It carries your selection straight into the quote, so we are pricing the finish you actually looked at.

03

Fabricate

Cut, brake-formed and assembled in the shop. Louvre spacing, crown profile and skirt depth are set per job, not pulled from a stock size. Long runs are formed to length so the finished work carries as few joints as possible.

04

Pick Up

We call as soon as your order is wrapped, labelled and staged. Collection is from our Burnaby shop. We supply trades right across the Lower Mainland, but finished work leaves from our door rather than going out to site, so allow for a pickup in your schedule. Touch up field cuts and scratches on install; the material pages on this site cover the maintenance each finish needs.
