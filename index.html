Who We Are

Esther’s Sheet Metal

We fabricate architectural sheet metal in Burnaby, British Columbia, and have done since 1995.

Most of what leaves the shop started as a drawing somebody brought us. Dimensions, louvre spacing and crown profiles change from one chimney to the next, so the parts are made for the job rather than pulled off a shelf.
