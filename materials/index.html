Copper

A finish that keeps working after you leave

Copper is the only material here that is not finished when it is installed. Scrub the timeline, or press play, to watch a standing seam cap move from mill bright through russet and chocolate brown to full verdigris.

Weathering rate depends on rainfall, orientation, air chemistry and how much of the surface is sheltered. Coastal and industrial exposures reach verdigris faster; a protected face under a deep overhang can stay brown for decades. Pre-patinated copper is available where matching an existing installation matters more than watching the change.
